Account Deletion Request Instructions for Toximeter Users

At Toximeter, we respect your privacy and provide you with the ability to request the deletion of your account and associated data. Below, you will find instructions on how to submit a deletion request, along with details on the types of data affected and our retention policy.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request the deletion of your account and personal data, please send an email to Toximeter.info@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the following details:

Your registered email address or Google Play account used for login.
A short confirmation stating that you wish to delete your account and associated data.
Once we receive your request, we will process it within 7 business days and confirm the deletion via email.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

Your profile information (email, username, and any personal details associated with your account).
Any app-related activity logs stored on our servers.
Advertising identifiers linked to your account.
Additionally, we use Appodeal for advertising, which may collect and store anonymous usage data. Please refer to Appodeal's privacy policy (https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy/) to learn how to manage or request deletion of advertising-related data.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain data may be retained for compliance with legal and security purposes, including:

Transaction records (if applicable) for accounting and fraud prevention.
Aggregated, non-personal analytics data used for service improvement (not linked to any specific user).
Any data required to comply with legal or regulatory obligations.
All retained data is securely stored and will be automatically deleted once it is no longer required.

If you have any further questions regarding data deletion, feel free to contact us at Toximeter.info@gmail.com.
